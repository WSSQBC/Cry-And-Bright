##Cry & Bright

**Listen to the shape of the universe.**

Cry & Bright is a bilingual incremental game about sound, resonance, echoes, and cosmic cycles. Tune frequencies, build echo chains, discover distant signals, and carry progress across resets.

## Features

- Tune and emit waves across the playable 1–30,000 Hz range. Frequency Expansion gradually raises the emission limit.
- Match harmonic ratios to trigger resonance bonuses, or manage interference between close frequencies.
- Upgrade wave amplitude, polyphony, resonance, decay, Breath recovery, and production.
- Unlock automated sound sources, soundscapes, permanent upgrades, relics, achievements, and expedition tiers.
- Follow milestone-based story chapters and uncover persistent deep-space discoveries.

##鸣与明

**聆听宇宙的形状。**

《鸣与明》是一款围绕声音、共鸣、回响与宇宙轮回展开的双语增量游戏。调节频率、构建回声链、发现远方信号，并在轮回中保留成长。

## 游戏特色

- 在可用的 1–30,000 Hz 范围内调频与发声；通过“频率拓展”逐步提高发射上限。
- 匹配谐波比例获得共鸣加成，并管理相近频率之间的干涉。
- 升级波幅、复音、共鸣、衰减、余息恢复与产出。
- 解锁自动声源、声场、永久升级、遗物、成就与远征层级。
- 随里程碑推进剧情章节，并发现可永久保存的深空记录。
